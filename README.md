# Lex-Vellum
LexVellum Differential is a sophisticated, AI-driven compliance engine designed to solve
the Compliance Paradox faced by international e-commerce leaders. operates a high-growth global storefront that requires a single,
unified Terms of Service  (ToS) document to satisfy a fragmented legal landscape.
Currently, QuantCart struggles to reconcile the strict privacy mandates of the GDPR (EU),
the transparency requirements of the CCPA (California), and the local regulations of UAE
Federal Law.
By utilizing a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture, LexVellum performs a
parallel Differential Analysis across these jurisdictions. Central to this solution is
the Immutable Audit Trail—a permanent  Truth Log that records every legal modification.
This ensures that every change made to satisfy a law is documented, providing Palwasha with
a historical defense during government audits. The system solves QuantCarts primary pain
points—high legal fees and launch delays—by providing Multi-Dimensional Risk
Analytics and Automated Redlining, ensuring she can scale her business with Global
Ambition  while remaining securely anchored in ;Local Regulation.
